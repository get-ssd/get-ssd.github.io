What it does

Sign your own posts and documents

The SSD app adds a compact Ed25519 signature to your posts, so readers running Tick can see it's really you. It also signs, seals and verifies whole documents as .ssd files, which you can encrypt to a named recipient.

Boosts and flags what you read

Tick checks every signed post on the page against the keys you hold. Attributable, genuine posts get a boost. Anonymous and untrusted posts don't, and posts that were tampered with get flagged. Nothing leaves your browser.

No central key authority

People put their public key in their profile on any social media platform, and Tick picks it up from there with one tap. You can also share keys by QR code, file or link. There is no registry, no gatekeeper and no account.

Desktop and mobile

Tick works in Firefox, Chrome, Edge and Brave on desktop, and on Android through Firefox Nightly. It works on Facebook, X, Reddit, Telegram and WhatsApp.

Download for Chrome
Installing Tick in Chrome
  1. Download the zip and unzip it to a folder you will keep.
  2. Go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
Installing Tick on Android (Firefox Nightly)
  1. Install Firefox Nightly from the Play Store. Regular Firefox for Android won't highlight posts on social media.
  2. Download the .xpi on your phone.
  3. In Nightly, go to Settings → About Firefox Nightly and tap the logo five times to turn on the debug menu.
  4. Back in Settings, tap Install add-on from file and pick the .xpi.
